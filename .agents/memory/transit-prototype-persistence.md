name: Transit prototype persistence
description: Product constraints for stored rider submissions and responses
---

Actual text-only AI summaries grounded in resident input and verified transit snapshots are authorized by the later public-data brief. Images still remain browser-local; this does not authorize sending photos to AI or persisting their contents.

**Why:** The public-data brief explicitly supersedes the earlier prewritten response requirement, but retains existing features rather than redesigning the product. The original browser-local image privacy constraint has not changed.

**How to apply:** Generate grounded text responses without treating a resident's reported problem as independently verified. Keep historical responses and source snapshots stable in History. Ask before adding photo transfer, analysis, or persistent image storage.
